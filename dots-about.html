Our story

How DOTS started.

DOTS began with electronic voting — clickers in a room, then software — a way to count votes quickly and clearly.

The rooms taught us that counting a vote was only one part of the work. Members also needed to know where to go, what was being decided, and how their participation would be handled.

That changed the role DOTS plays. We became a meeting partner as much as a technology provider: helping organizations introduce tools in a way that feels calm, credible, and usable when the decision matters.

Over 16+ years, DOTS grew from clickers and services into DOTS Connect: the member portal that keeps access, participation, and the final record connected.

The guiding idea

Technology earns trust when it makes participation feel clearer, not more complicated.

That means designing around the people in the room, the procedure they follow, and the decision they need to make together.
