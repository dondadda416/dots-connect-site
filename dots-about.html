About DOTS

The team behind the technology.

Data On The Spot has supported member meetings and electronic voting since 2009.

From clickers to DOTS Connect.

We started by helping organizations count votes in a room.

Working at live events showed us how much happens around the ballot: preparing members, explaining the process, managing discussion, and recording decisions.

That experience shaped DOTS Connect and the event support we provide today.
