One connected participation journey

One meeting rhythm, before, during, and after.

Before

Before the agenda opens.

Give every member a clear starting place before the live meeting begins.

  • Early access, credentials, and materials
  • Practice, questions, and defined pre-meeting decisions
  • Clear reminders and opening instructions
Live meeting

During the meeting.

Participation stays orderly and visible as the agenda moves forward.

  • Speaker queue and moderated Q&A
  • Motions, amendments, and voting when needed
  • Quorum and live meeting status
After

After the decision.

Results and next steps remain easy to find once the meeting is over.

  • Real-time results and reporting
  • Published outcomes and next steps
  • Past meetings, materials, and records

The member portal

The member home for a meeting in motion.

When the meeting is underway, DOTS Connect is where members find the agenda, materials, and the right way to take part. No app or download required.

  • Agenda & schedule — the whole meeting at a glance.
  • Materials & resources — documents, reports, and financials in reach.
  • Speaker queue & Q&A — members raise a hand and take part.
  • Registration & credentials — the right people, verified.
  • Live voting when needed — the ballot, right where the meeting is.

Formal business, handled

Support for the moments where formal meetings get complicated.

Manage motions, amendments, and discussion in order — so procedure supports the meeting instead of derailing it.

  • Motions & resolutions — presented clearly, with full text.
  • Amendments in sequence — tracked as they're proposed.
  • Move to discussion — structured debate, not chaos.
  • Thresholds & majorities — the right bar for each decision.
  • A clear record — what was decided, and how.

Zoom integration
