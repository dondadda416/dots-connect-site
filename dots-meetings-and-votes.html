Meetings & Events

Keep the agenda moving.

Give delegates the documents, discussion tools, and live ballots they need for an AGM, convention, or town hall.

Four tools for the chair and members.

Agenda & documents

Publish the schedule, reports, and supporting materials before the meeting. Members can return to them throughout the session.

Moderated Q&A

Collect questions ahead of time or during the meeting. Hosts review submissions before sharing them with the presenter.

Speaker queue

Members request the floor and see their place. The chair calls speakers in order and manages the discussion.

Resolutions & amendments

Show the full text under consideration and track proposed amendments as business moves through debate and voting.
